# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 27, 2026

> **Status:** the Tutor plan is not on sale yet. Until it launches, Landmark Prep has no accounts and takes no payments, and your study progress stays in your browser. Our web host keeps only standard request logs. The sections about accounts, the Tutor and billing describe how the plan will work when it opens.

This Privacy Policy explains how Landmark Prep, operated by KE Studios (New York, USA), collects, uses, and shares information when you use our online real estate license exam prep service.

We wrote this in plain English on purpose. If something is unclear, email william@kestudios.dev.

## 1. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the service.

**Account email.** When you sign up, we store the email address you give us. We use it to identify your account, send login and billing notices, and answer support requests.

**Progress data.** We store which units you have opened, which practice questions you have answered, and similar study progress so that you can pick up where you left off on another device.

**Tutor conversations.** If you use the optional Tutor plan, we store the messages you send and the replies you receive so the tutor can stay in context and so we can operate, debug, and improve that feature.

**Payment information.** Paid plans are billed through Stripe. Stripe processes your payment method. We never see full card numbers. We may receive limited billing status from Stripe, such as whether a charge succeeded, the last four digits of a card, and the current subscription state.

**Technical logs.** Our servers may record IP address, browser type, and timestamps needed to keep the site secure and working. We do not use these logs to build advertising profiles.

## 2. How we use information

We use the information above to:

- Create and maintain your account.
- Deliver free study content and, if you subscribe, Tutor access.
- Remember your progress.
- Process monthly Tutor charges of $34.99 through Stripe.
- Respond when you write to william@kestudios.dev.
- Detect abuse, fix bugs, and keep the service available.
- Comply with law when we are legally required to do so.

We do not use your study answers or tutor chats for advertising, and we do not use them to train AI models. We do not sell personal data. We do not sell, rent, or trade your email, progress, or conversations to data brokers or advertisers.

## 3. Cookies

Landmark Prep uses essential cookies only. These cookies keep you signed in, protect against fraud, and remember basic site settings. We do not place advertising cookies, and we do not run third-party ad networks on the study pages.

You can block cookies in your browser. If you block essential cookies, some features (including staying logged in) may not work.

## 4. Who we share information with

We share information only with:

- **Stripe**, to process payments. Stripe has its own privacy policy. We never see full card numbers.
- **Our AI model provider (Anthropic)**, which receives your tutor messages and the study context needed to write each reply. It processes them to answer you and does not use them to train its models.
- **Hosting and infrastructure providers** that store our application and databases under contract, solely to run Landmark Prep.
- **Authorities**, if a valid legal demand requires it.

We do not sell personal data to anyone.

## 5. How long we keep it

We keep account email and progress data for as long as the account exists. Tutor conversations are kept while they are needed to provide the Tutor feature and for a reasonable period afterward for support and security. If you close your account or ask us to delete your data, we remove or anonymize personal information unless the law requires us to keep a record (for example, a billing receipt).

## 6. Your choices

You may:

- Update the email on your account.
- Cancel a Tutor plan from the account page at any time.
- Request an export of the personal data we hold about you.
- Request deletion of your account and personal data.

To request an export or to delete your data, email william@kestudios.dev from the address on the account. We will verify the request and then provide a copy or delete the data as described above.

If you live in a jurisdiction with additional privacy rights (for example, the right to correct inaccurate data or to limit certain processing), we will honor those rights when they apply. Say which right you want to exercise when you write to us.

## 7. Children

Landmark Prep is meant for adults preparing for a professional license. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact william@kestudios.dev and we will delete it.

## 8. Security

We use standard safeguards such as encrypted connections and access controls. No online service is perfectly secure. Please choose a strong password and keep it private.

## 9. International users

KE Studios is based in New York, USA. If you use Landmark Prep from outside the United States, your information is processed in the United States.

## 10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The date at the top will change when we do. If we make a material change to how we use personal data, we will post the update on this page.

## 11. Contact

Landmark Prep is operated by KE Studios. For privacy questions, export requests, or deletion requests, write to william@kestudios.dev.
