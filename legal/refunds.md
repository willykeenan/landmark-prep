# Cancellation and Refund Policy

**Last updated:** September 27, 2026

> **Status:** Landmark Prep has no paid plan and takes no payments. It has no accounts, and your study progress stays in your browser. Our web host keeps only standard request logs. The "Ask ChatGPT" and "Ask Claude" buttons open your own account with those services, and what you type there is governed by their terms, not ours. The sections about accounts, the Tutor plan and billing describe how a paid plan would work if we ever offer one.

This Cancellation and Refund Policy explains how billing, cancellation, and refunds work for Landmark Prep, the online real estate license exam prep service operated by KE Studios.

Study content is free. You can read national exam notes, use the question banks, and review state licensing facts without paying. This policy applies to the optional paid Tutor plan.

If you have a billing question, email william@kestudios.dev.

## 1. The Tutor plan

The optional Tutor plan costs $34.99 per month in US dollars. Stripe bills that amount each month until you cancel. Taxes may appear as a separate line if the law requires them.

You authorize Landmark Prep (through Stripe) to charge the payment method on file for each monthly period when you start a Tutor plan.

## 2. How to cancel

You may cancel anytime from the account page. Open your account settings, choose the Tutor plan, and confirm cancellation. You do not need to call or write a letter. If the account page is unavailable, email william@kestudios.dev from the address on the account and we will cancel the plan for you.

Canceling stops future monthly charges. It does not delete your account or your free study progress.

## 3. Access after you cancel

When you cancel, paid Tutor access continues to the end of the period you already paid for. For example, if Stripe billed you on the 10th and you cancel on the 20th, Tutor access remains through the end of that paid month. After that date, the Tutor feature turns off. Free study content stays available.

## 4. No pro-rated refunds for partial months

We do not issue pro-rated refunds for unused days in a billing period. If you cancel in the middle of a month, you keep Tutor access until that paid period ends, and we do not refund a pro-rated share of $34.99 for the remaining days. Please cancel before the next renewal if you do not want another charge.

This no pro-rated refund rule is the standard rule for every month after the first charge.

## 5. First-charge refund (7 days)

We will refund your first Tutor charge on request if you ask within 7 days of that first charge. Write to william@kestudios.dev from the email on your account, include the date of the charge if you have it, and ask for a first-charge refund. We will refund the first $34.99 charge through Stripe.

The 7-day first-charge refund is a goodwill policy for new subscribers. It applies only to the first charge on the account, not to later monthly renewals. After 7 days, that first charge follows the same no pro-rated refund rule as later months.

## 6. Later charges

Renewal charges after the first month are not refundable except where we are legally required to refund them, or where we charged you after a successful cancel and the extra charge was our error. If a charge posts after you canceled in time, email william@kestudios.dev and we will refund that mistaken charge.

## 7. Failed payments

If Stripe cannot collect a monthly payment, Tutor access may pause until the payment succeeds. We may retry the charge according to Stripe’s retry rules. You can update the payment method on the account page. If payments keep failing, the Tutor plan may end; free study content remains.

## 8. Chargebacks

If you dispute a charge with your bank or card issuer, we may pause Tutor access while we review the dispute. Contact william@kestudios.dev first so we can help without a chargeback when a simple refund or cancel is the right fix.

## 9. Price changes

If the $34.99 monthly price changes, we will email you at least 30 days before the new price applies to your account. A price change does not alter the cancel rules, the end-of-period access rule, the no pro-rated refunds rule, or the 7-day first-charge refund for people who are still in that window.

## 10. Free content

Canceling or requesting a refund does not remove your access to free Landmark Prep study materials. Notes, question banks, and state facts stay available under the Terms of Service.

## 11. Contact

For cancellation help or a first-charge refund request, email william@kestudios.dev. KE Studios operates Landmark Prep from New York, USA.
